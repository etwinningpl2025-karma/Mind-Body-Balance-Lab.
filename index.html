Background & Objectives

The project “Mind & Body Balance Lab” is designed as an international collaboration between secondary schools focused on exploring the relationship between mental health and healthy lifestyle habits among adolescents. Through joint activities, students analyse how everyday routines such as sleep, nutrition, physical activity, and digital media influence emotional state, stress levels, and overall well-being.

Overall Aim

To develop students’ awareness, knowledge, and skills for protecting mental health through the adoption of healthy lifestyle habits, strengthening emotional and social competences, and reducing stigma.

Specific Aims

  • Distinguish scientific information from myths regarding mental health.
  • Analyze connection between sleep, nutrition, physical activity, and well-being.
  • Apply stress-management strategies and micro-interventions.
  • Collaborate in international teams using digital tools.

8-Phase Project Roadmap (October → May)

October

Phase 1: Project launch and mental health literacy

Meet partners, complete baseline surveys, explore mental health concepts and myths.

November

Phase 2: Sleep and mental wellbeing

Investigate sleep, concentration, and mood; develop healthy sleep habits.

December

Phase 3: Nutrition and physical activity

Explore lifestyle and wellbeing; create practical healthy habit resources.

January

Phase 4: Stress management and emotional regulation

Learn and practise micro-interventions, emotional awareness, and coping strategies.

February

Phase 5: Digital balance and social wellbeing

Reflect on digital habits, social connection, and healthy boundaries.

March

Phase 6: International teamwork and toolkit creation

Combine partner contributions and draft the Student Wellbeing Toolkit.

April

Phase 7: Mental health awareness campaign

Produce posters, digital campaign materials, and dissemination activities.

May

Phase 8: Finalisation, evaluation and celebration

Complete the toolkit, evaluate learning, share results, and close the project.
